# Application Email Rules & Constraints

## 1. Candidate Location & Relocation Phrasing (STRICT)
- **NEVER use the phrase "based in [City]"** (e.g. do not say "based in Hyderabad", "based in Bangalore", or "based in Chennai"), as the word "based" implies native residency.
- **For Hyderabad Requirements:** Strictly state:
  `- Immediate joiner, ready to relocate to Hyderabad.`
- **For Bangalore Requirements:** Strictly state:
  `- Immediate joiner, ready to relocate to Bangalore.`
- **For Chennai Requirements:** Strictly state:
  `- Immediate joiner, ready to relocate to Chennai.`
- **For General / Remote / Multi-City:** Strictly state:
  `- Immediate joiner, ready to relocate immediately.`

## 2. Salutation & Greeting Line
- Strictly: `Dear Hiring Team,`
- Never append company name suffix (e.g., never say "Dear Hiring Team at [Company],").

## 3. Formatting & Bullet Styles
- Plain hyphens (`-`) only for all bullet points.
- Zero emojis (🚀, ⭐, etc.) and zero checkmark symbols (`✓`, `✔`).

## 4. Experience & Profile Integrity
- Maintain authentic ~2 years Frontend Developer experience at Youngminds (Dec 2024 – July 2026).
- Prior foundation: Business Process Associate at Accenture (Aug 2023 – Nov 2024).
- Key Projects: PastryChef ERP (offline-first POS desktop application with local-to-server sync & tax workflows) and ETC Mentors.
- Core Stack: React.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, Electron.js, Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap, REST APIs.
- Do not inflate experience.

## 5. Attachments & Deduplication
- Always attach: `Jyoshna_Rayadurgam_Frontend_Developer.pdf`.
- Always check and log every dispatch against `sent_history.json`.
- Anti-spam jitter pacing: 4–6s between emails.
